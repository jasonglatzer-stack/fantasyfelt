How it works

01

Spend the cap

Every pro in the GPI top 1,000 has a price: 60% GPI ranking, 40% recent WSOP bracelet-event form, on a curve from 1,000 down to 1. You get 2,500.

02

Lock up to 8 before Event #1

Stars or depth, your call. Every pick you add is another player who can score. Lineups lock when the $2,750 Mystery Bounty starts on December 2 (11:00 AM in Nassau). No swaps after that.

03

Sweat the series

Your picks score in all 20 bracelet events. The leaderboard updates daily through the $15K Last Chance on December 17.
