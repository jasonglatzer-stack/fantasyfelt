Contest rules

FantasyFelt · WSOP Paradise 2026 Beta. Last updated November 24, 2026.

Free, for pride only

  • Entry is free. There is no purchase, deposit or payment of any kind.
  • There are no prizes. The contest is played for pride only. The winner gets bragging rights and a place in the FantasyFelt Hall of Felt.
  • FantasyFelt is in beta. Features may change between events, but the rules for this contest are fixed once entries open.

Who can enter

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • One team per person and per email address. Duplicate or automated entries may be removed.
  • Team names must be 2–30 characters and must not be offensive, impersonate anyone, or contain links. We may rename or remove teams that break this rule.

Building a team

  • Pick up to 8 players (at least 1) from the FantasyFelt player pool (the Global Poker Index top 1,000).
  • Each player has a price from 1 to 1,000. Your players can cost at most 2,500 in total.
  • Prices are frozen for this contest. They are based 60% on GPI ranking (Nov 18, 2026 update) and 40% on WSOP bracelet-event results since 2024 (wsop.com, Nov 23, 2026).
  • Lineups lock at the start of Event #1 on December 2, 2026 at 11:00 AM in Nassau (16:00 UTC). Teams cannot be changed after you submit or after lock.

Scoring

  • All 20 bracelet events of WSOP Paradise 2026 count, including Event #5, the WSOP Pro-Am.
  • A player must cash to score. Finish points: 1st 50, 2nd 45, 3rd 40, 4th 35, 5th 30, 6th 25, 7th 20, 8th 15, 9th 10, 10th–18th 5, any other cash 1.
  • Field bonus: top-18 finishers get +1 point per 100 entries. Nine more places qualify for the bonus per 1,000 entries.
  • Multipliers by buy-in: $2,750 events 1×, $5,300 events 1.5×, $10,000 and up 2×, the $26,000 Super Main Event 3×.
  • Players who share a place in heads-up or shootout rounds split that place's points, rounded up.
  • Results come from the official wsop.com payouts. If wsop.com corrects a result, we correct the scores.
  • Ties: best single-event score, then most cashes, then the earliest entry.

General

  • FantasyFelt is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the WSOP, GGPoker, Baha Mar or any player.
  • We may correct obvious errors and remove entries that break these rules. Our decision on scoring is final.
  • Questions: contact us through fantasyfelt.com.
