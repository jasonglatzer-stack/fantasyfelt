Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

Who we are

FantasyFelt (fantasyfelt.com) is run for poker players, by poker players. It is in beta, and fuller details about who runs it will be added here as it grows.

What we collect and why

  • Email address and team name, to run the contest: one team per email, contacting winners about the Hall of Felt, and fixing problems with your entry. Legal basis: performing the contest you entered.
  • Your lineup, to score the contest. Your team name and players are shown publicly on the leaderboard after lineups lock. Your email never is.
  • Marketing emails, only if you tick the optional box. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the unsubscribe link in every email.

Where it is stored

Entries are stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Marketing emails are sent with Brevo (EU). The site is hosted on Cloudflare. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Contest entries are kept for 24 months after the contest ends so we can run the Hall of Felt and answer questions. Marketing consent lasts until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by contacting us (details above). You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Lithuania, the State Data Protection Inspectorate).

Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser keeps your unfinished lineup locally so it is not lost if you reload the page.
